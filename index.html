Clean Tech & Decarbonisation

Rationale

A data-driven project measuring household energy transformation, optimization, and real-world return on investments.

Energy transition is increasingly linked to the cost of living crisis so reducing cost – and showing cost can be reduced - is urgent.

Government policy is moving towards some homeowners making personal investment to drive transition (combined with grants), so good predictions on returns on investment are required to persuade people to invest.

Storage is expensive and the grid required updating, so using power when and where its generated is needed.

Project Foundations

Optimisation Factors

Optimisation of consumption (cost) is a complex undertaking with multiple factors to take into account including:

Weather

Unpredictable solar generation and varying heat loads demand ambient weather intelligence.

Home Appliance Differences

Varied load cycles, non-linear wattage curves, and peak consumption signatures across devices.

Personal Needs and Goals

Balancing comfort preferences, daily routines, and financial budget thresholds.

Our Project & Components

Project Goal

To gather as much data as can easily be accessed from a retrofitted home, using mostly commodity devices, with a view to a next stage of being able to make forecasts, predictions and automations.

What We Did

  • Octopus API: Used for electricity import/export monitoring
  • Tapo Smart Plugs: Energy measuring plugs on key appliances
  • YoLink Sensors: Temperature and humidity sensors throughout home
  • Dashboard & Insights: Live monitoring and initial analytical insights

Strategic Automation

Insights: Timeshifting Scenarios

Timeshifting needed to use self-generated electricity. The most efficient timings will vary according to weather, time-of-day tariffs and patterns of individual appliances.

Scenario 1: No Scheduling

Unoptimized

High load spikes occur during off-peak solar production (e.g. early morning or night), forcing grid imports.

Scenario 2: Time Shift

Optimized

Loads are shifted directly into the solar generation bell-curve, maximizing self-consumption and reducing import bills.

Future Horizons & Seasonal Strategy

Winter Strategy: Shifting to Low-Tariff Hours

Solar Generation Curves vs. Octopus Cosy Tariff "Triple Dip"

Comparing Summer & Winter solar yield curves with highlighted off-peak low-tariff windows (p/kWh).

Summer Solar Winter Solar Cosy Tariff Cheap Energy Dips
Winter Adjustment Requirement

Because winter solar yield drops significantly (reaching only ~500W at mid-day peak), afternoon solar timeshifting alone is no longer sufficient. Heavy loads should be shifted into the highlighted "Triple Dip" low-tariff windows (04:00–07:00, 13:00–16:00, and 22:00–00:00) to run Heat Pump hot water cycles, Dishwasher, and Washing Machine at minimal cost while avoiding the expensive 16:00–19:00 peak rate.
